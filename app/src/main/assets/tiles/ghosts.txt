# Pintley tiles - Ghosts
# One prompt per line. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
# Use "Player X", "Player Y", "Player Z" - they become random player names.
# Keep prompts short so they read at a glance on screen.
#
# Ghosts haunt a player: each ghost adds +1 to every drink that player takes.
# They stack up and stay until something banishes them.
#
# Balance: for every 1 banishing card there should be roughly
# 3 one-ghost cards, 2 two-ghost cards and 1 three-ghost card.
#
# Lines starting with [requires: Category] only appear once that category has
# already come up in the game - so you never banish ghosts nobody has.

# --- Gain 1 ghost ---
Player X, you have found a ghost
Player X, something followed you home. Take a ghost
Player X and Player Y, you both take a ghost
Whoever is least haunted takes a ghost
Everyone point at who seems most haunted. They take a ghost
Player X, name a player. They take a ghost, you take one too

# --- Gain 2 ghosts ---
Player X, you are haunted. Take 2 ghosts
Player X, you slept in a graveyard. Take 2 ghosts
Player X, the seance worked. Take 2 ghosts
Last player to drink takes 2 ghosts

# --- Gain 3 ghosts (the big ones) ---
Player X, the whole graveyard followed you home. Take 3 ghosts
Player X, you are cursed. Take 3 ghosts

# --- Banishing ---
[requires: Ghosts][clears: Ghosts] Sunrise! Every ghost is banished from the game
[requires: Ghosts][transient] Player X, you have been cleansed. Lose all your ghosts

# --- Moving them around / making them hurt ---
[requires: Ghosts][transient] Player X, pass one of your ghosts to another player
[requires: Ghosts][transient] Player X, swap all your ghosts with Player Y
[requires: Ghosts][transient] Player X, drink your ghost count
[requires: Ghosts][transient] Everyone drinks their ghost count
