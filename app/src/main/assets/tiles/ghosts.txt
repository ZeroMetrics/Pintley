# Pintley tiles - Ghosts
# One prompt per line. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
# Use "Player X", "Player Y", "Player Z" - they become random player names.
# Keep prompts short so they read at a glance on screen.
#
# Ghosts haunt a player: each ghost adds +1 to every drink that player takes.
# They stack up and stay until something banishes them.
#
# The app keeps the running count, so every card here must name exactly who is
# affected. Avoid "give a ghost to another player" or "whoever is least haunted"
# - the app can't tell who that is, and the count would drift out of sync.
#
# [ghosts: N] gives N ghosts to each player named on the card.
# [ghosts: clear] the named player loses theirs. [ghosts: clearall] wipes everyone.
# [ghosts: transfer] moves one ghost from the first named player to the second.
# [ghosts: swap] swaps the two named players' counts.
#
# Balance: for every 1 banishing card there should be roughly
# 3 one-ghost cards, 2 two-ghost cards and 1 three-ghost card.

# --- Gain 1 ghost ---
[ghosts: 1] Player X, you have found a ghost
[ghosts: 1] Player X, something followed you home. Take a ghost
[ghosts: 1] Player X and Player Y, you both take a ghost
[ghosts: 1] Player X, you heard footsteps upstairs. Take a ghost
[ghosts: 1] Player X, the mirror blinked. Take a ghost
[ghosts: 1] Player X, you said its name out loud. Take a ghost

# --- Gain 2 ghosts ---
[ghosts: 2] Player X, you are haunted. Take 2 ghosts
[ghosts: 2] Player X, you slept in a graveyard. Take 2 ghosts
[ghosts: 2] Player X, the seance worked. Take 2 ghosts
[ghosts: 2] Player X, you opened the wrong door. Take 2 ghosts

# --- Gain 3 ghosts (the big ones) ---
[ghosts: 3] Player X, the whole graveyard followed you home. Take 3 ghosts
[ghosts: 3] Player X, you are cursed. Take 3 ghosts

# --- Banishing ---
[requires: Ghosts][clears: Ghosts][ghosts: clearall] Sunrise! Every ghost is banished from the game
[requires: Ghosts][transient][ghosts: clear] Player X, you have been cleansed. Lose all your ghosts

# --- Moving them around / making them hurt ---
[requires: Ghosts][transient][ghosts: transfer] Player X, pass a ghost to Player Y
[requires: Ghosts][transient][ghosts: swap] Player X, swap ghosts with Player Y
[requires: Ghosts][transient] Player X, drink your ghost count
[requires: Ghosts][transient] Everyone drinks their ghost count
