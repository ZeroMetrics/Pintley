# Pintley tiles - Name That Bird
# One prompt per line. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
# Use "Player X", "Player Y", "Player Z" - they become random player names.
# Keep prompts short so they read at a glance on screen.
# There must be more lines here than there are photos in assets/birds/, or the
# prompts run out before the birds do and some birds never get shown.

Player X, name that bird. If correct give out 3 drinks. If wrong take 3 drinks
Player Y, name that bird. Correct and you hand out 4 drinks, wrong and you take 4
Player Z, name that bird. Get it right and everyone else drinks 2
Player X, name that bird. Miss it and you drink 4
Player Y, name that bird. Correct and you choose someone to down half their drink
Player Z, name that bird. Wrong answer costs you 2 and a ghost
Player X, name that bird. Nail it and you give out 5 drinks
Player Y, name that bird. Wrong and you take 3, right and Player Z takes 3
Player Z, name that bird. Correct and you are safe from the next card
Player X, name that bird. Every player who thinks they know it can join in, wrong guesses drink 2
Player Y, name that bird. Wrong and everyone points at you and you drink 3
Player Z, name that bird. Get it right and make a rule for the next 3 cards
Player X, name that bird. Wrong and Player Y picks your punishment
Player Y, name that bird. Correct and you skip your next drink of any size
Player Z, name that bird. Wrong and you drink 2, then guess again for double
Player X, name that bird. First person to shout the right name gives out 3 drinks
Player Y, name that bird. Wrong and the group decides how many you drink, up to 5
Player Z, name that bird. Correct and the loudest doubter drinks 4
